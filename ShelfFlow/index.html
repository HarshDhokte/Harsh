02Secondary research

Secondary research insights

Published studies show how far store records drift from the shelf.

03Primary research

Primary research insights

Store visits to Yousta, Mr. DIY and Star Bazaar, manager and staff interviews, and a survey of 26 shoppers.

  1. 01

    A missed scan hides an item from sale. An unscanned item reads as zero at the till.

  2. 02

    An item takes about three days to become sellable. Stock corrections run on paper sheets.

  3. 03

    Alerts reach the manager, and the floor waits. Low-designation staff work outside the app.

  4. 04

    Stores invest in tech that shoppers see first. Staff tools get the least attention.

09Build

A working shelf feeds both apps live.

IR sensors on an Arduino read each lane. A YOLOv8n model trained on three products identifies each pack. A FastAPI backend updates both apps in one to two seconds.
